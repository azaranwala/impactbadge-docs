Gov/K12 Domain Verification

Enable government agencies and school staff to create volunteer events — deployed September 2026

Problem Solved

Previously, only 501(c)(3) nonprofits could create events on ImpactBadge (via IRS EIN lookup). This excluded parks departments, public libraries, county programs, teachers, and school counselors — all of whom regularly organize volunteer opportunities.

3 Layers of Student Protection

Since students also have .k12 email addresses, three safeguards prevent them from registering as event creators:

Layer 1: Student Subdomain Blocking (Backend)

Automatically rejects email domains starting with known student prefixes:

PrefixExampleResult
student.student.hcps.netBLOCKED
stu.stu.fcps.eduBLOCKED
s.s.henrico.k12.va.usBLOCKED
students.students.school.orgBLOCKED
learner.learner.district.eduBLOCKED

Layer 2: Staff Role Validation (Backend)

For .k12 and .edu domains, the contactRole field is required and must contain a recognized staff keyword:

Accepted: Teacher, Principal, Counselor, Librarian, Coach, Dean, Coordinator, Advisor, Instructor, Specialist, Superintendent, Director, Administrator, Faculty, Staff

Rejected: Student, Volunteer, Member, or anything under 3 characters

Government .gov domains skip this check (park rangers, librarians, etc. have unique titles).

Layer 3: UI Gating (iOS + Android)

The "Register Your Agency" button is hidden from users who:

  • Already have role: org_admin (already registered)
  • Have schoolName set (enrolled students auto-detected via school domain)

Only users who are NOT enrolled students see the button.
