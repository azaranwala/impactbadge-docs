One-Link Nonprofit Onboarding

Reduce nonprofit onboarding from 6 steps to 3 clicks — deployed September 2026

The Problem

Previously, onboarding a nonprofit required 6 steps across 2 platforms: download app → create account → navigate to organizations → search → claim → verify email. This created massive friction and killed GTM conversion.

The Solution

A GTM lead generates a single shareable link (with QR code) for a specific nonprofit. The nonprofit contact clicks it, enters their name + email, and gets verified — all without downloading the app.

End-to-End Flow

GTM Lead (You)

1
Log in to impacthub-prod.web.app as super_admin
2
Navigate to Nonprofit Onboarding in the sidebar
3
Click "Generate Invite Link" → search for the nonprofit
4
Optionally check "This is a local chapter" and enter chapter name
5
Get the link + QR code. Copy or screenshot. Send via email/text.
6
Track status on the dashboard: Generated → Email Submitted → Verified → First Event Created

Nonprofit Contact

1
Clicks the link (or scans QR code)
2
Sees branded page with org name pre-filled + benefit list
3
Enters name + email → clicks "Accept & Verify Email"
4
Gets verification email → clicks verify link
5
Account created automatically → becomes org_admin
6
Downloads app, signs in, creates first event

Chapter Support (Bottom-Up Adoption)

For organizations with multiple local chapters (e.g., Habitat for Humanity, Boys & Girls Club):

  • GTM lead checks "This is a local chapter" when generating the invite
  • Chapter contact becomes chapter_admin (not org_owner)
  • Multiple chapter invites allowed per EIN (each with different chapter name)
  • Chapter invites work even if the parent org is already claimed
  • Parent org created automatically if it doesn't exist yet

Use case: North South Foundation has chapters in Richmond, Virginia Beach, and Norfolk. Each chapter gets its own invite link and onboards independently.

Automated Reminder Emails

After a nonprofit verifies, automated emails encourage them to create their first event:

Reminders auto-stop when the org creates their first event. The invite status updates to "First Event Created".

Self-Serve Org Signup (Alternative Path)

In addition to GTM-generated invite links, organizations can sign up directly at /signup/org without an invitation:

  • 4 org types supported: Nonprofit, School, Library, Parks & Recreation
  • EIN validation for nonprofits; domain validation for schools (.k12/.edu)
  • If EIN/domain cannot be auto-verified, submission goes to orgReviewQueue for manual review
  • Cloud Function: selfServeOrgSignupCall

This enables organic sign-ups without requiring a GTM lead to generate a link first.
