Nonprofit & Org Admin Guide

Set up your organization, create events, verify hours, and generate impact reports

Getting Started — Two Ways to Join

Option A: Self-Serve Signup (New — No Invite Needed)

Organizations can now sign up directly at impactbadge.app/signup/org — no invitation or referral required.

1. Go to /signup/org

Visit impactbadge.app/signup/org and select your organization type.

2. Choose Your Org Type

ImpactBadge supports 4 organization types: Nonprofit (validated by EIN), School (validated by .edu/.k12 domain), Library, and Parks & Rec (validated by domain or submitted for manual review).

3. Enter Your Details

Organization name, your name, email, and EIN or domain for validation. If automatic validation fails, your request is submitted to the manual review queue.

4. Get Verified

EIN and domain matches are approved instantly. Manual review requests are processed within 1 business day. You receive an email when approved.

Option B: Claim an Existing Organization

1. Create an Account

Sign up with your organization email (email/password or Google). Your name and email will be pre-filled in the claim form.

2. Search for Your Nonprofit

Go to "Nonprofit Search" (or Settings → "Claim Your Organization") → enter your organization name. We search the IRS nonprofit registry.

3. Claim Your Organization

Tap "Claim This Organization." Your name and email are pre-filled from your profile — just verify and tap "Submit Claim." If you represent a local chapter or branch, toggle "I represent a local chapter" and enter your chapter name (e.g., "Richmond, VA").

4. Verify via Email

Click the verification link in the email (expires in 7 days). Your account is upgraded to Org Admin (or Chapter Admin if you claimed a chapter).

5. Access Your Dashboard

After verification, you land on the Org Admin Dashboard with full event and volunteer management — scoped to your chapter if applicable.

Multi-Chapter / Branch Organizations

If your nonprofit has multiple chapters or branches (e.g., North South Foundation with 50+ state chapters), ImpactBadge supports independent chapter management under a single organization.

How It Works

  • Bottom-Up: Each chapter director can claim independently — no need to wait for the national org to join. Just toggle "I represent a local chapter" during the claim process.
  • Top-Down: If the national org is already on ImpactBadge, the org owner can create chapters and invite directors from the Chapters management page.
  • Independent Operation: Each chapter creates its own events, manages its own volunteers, and verifies its own hours.
  • National Visibility: When a national director joins (claiming the entire organization), they see all chapters, all events, and aggregated hours — perfect for grant reporting.

Managing Chapters (Org Owner)

1. Open Chapters

Tap the building icon on your dashboard (iOS) or go to the web portal at impacthub-prod.web.app/admin/chapters.

2. Create a Chapter

Tap "+" → enter chapter name and region (e.g., "Virginia" / "VA").

3. Invite a Director

Swipe on a chapter → "Invite Admin" → enter their email. They receive an invitation link.

4. Monitor All Chapters

Your analytics dashboard shows per-chapter breakdown: events, hours, volunteers for each chapter.

Organization Branding

Customize how your organization appears to students across ImpactBadge:

  • Logo URL — Upload your organization logo from the Admin Dashboard → Settings → "Organization Logo." Enter the URL of your logo image. This appears on event cards, your org profile, and impact reports.
  • Banner URL — Upload a banner image from Settings → "Organization Banner." This displays at the top of your organization's public profile page.
Recommended sizes: Logo — 200x200px (square, PNG or JPG). Banner — 1200x400px (landscape).

Creating Events

1. Tap "Create Event"

From your dashboard, tap the + button or "Create Event".

2. Fill in Event Details

Title, description, category (Environment, Education, etc.), date/time, address (with autocomplete), and maximum volunteer slots.

3. Add Banner Image (Optional)

Enter a banner image URL to display on the event card and detail page. Creates a visually compelling event listing that stands out in the student Explore feed.

4. Add Meeting Link for Virtual Events (Optional)

For virtual volunteer events, enter a meeting link (Zoom, Microsoft Teams, or Google Meet). The link replaces the physical address and is shown to registered volunteers. Students check in via PIN — no GPS required for virtual events.

5. Add Shifts (Optional)

For multi-shift events, add up to 10 shifts with different time slots and volunteer caps.

6. Publish

The event goes live immediately. Nearby students will see it on their Explore tab. A check-in PIN is auto-generated.

Calendar Sync for Attendees

After registering for your event, students can download a .ics file (from the web portal) or add the event directly to their device calendar (from the mobile app). The calendar entry includes the event title, time, location, and meeting link (for virtual events). This helps reduce no-shows by keeping your event on students' calendars.

Check-In PIN: Share this 4-digit code with volunteers who can't use GPS check-in. Find it on the event detail page.

Sharing Events on Social Media

Tap the share icon on any event detail page to share it on social media (Facebook, Instagram, LinkedIn, TikTok, WhatsApp, X, etc.). ImpactBadge generates a branded event card image (iOS) or formatted text (Android) with a link to a public event page:

  • Public event page: impacthub-prod.web.app/events/{eventId} — anyone can view event details without an account
  • Registration requires login: To sign up, users must download the app and create an account
  • Students can share too: After registering, students see a "Share my registration" button to tell friends

Managing Registrations

When students register for your event, you can:

  • View all registrations with status (Confirmed, Waitlisted, Checked In, Verified)
  • See how many spots are filled vs. available
  • Post announcements to all registered volunteers
  • Cancel the event (all registered volunteers are notified automatically)

Waitlist Auto-Promote

When a confirmed volunteer cancels their registration, the first person on the waitlist is automatically promoted to "Confirmed." They receive both a push notification and an email letting them know their spot has been secured. No manual action is needed from you — the system handles the promotion instantly.

Verifying Hours

Automatic Verification (GPS)

If a student checked in via GPS, their hours are auto-verified on checkout. No action needed from you. The student sees "Auto-Verified via GPS" and receives a certificate immediately.

Manual Verification (PIN/QR)

1. Check Pending Verifications

Dashboard → "Pending Verifications" shows all hours awaiting your approval.

2. Review Evidence

Click the evidence icon to see e-signatures and photos the student submitted.

3. Approve or Reject

Click "Approve" to verify hours or "Reject" if incorrect. Students are notified either way.

4. Bulk Approve

Select multiple pending verifications and approve them all at once.

Self-Reported Hours

Students can self-report hours for work done outside ImpactBadge. As the supervisor, you'll receive an email with a "Verify Hours" button. Click to approve or reject — no ImpactBadge account needed.

Impact Reports (Grant-Ready PDF)

1. Go to Analytics → "Generate Impact Report"

Select date range (defaults to last 6 months). Optionally include top 20 volunteers.

2. Generate Report

ImpactBadge compiles: total hours, unique volunteers, events hosted, repeat rate, check-in rate, category breakdown, monthly trends, and estimated community impact.

3. Receive PDF

A professional grant-ready PDF is emailed to your account and available for download. Includes your EIN and organization details.

Grant Season Tip: Generate reports in Q4 (October-December) and Q1 (January-March) when most grant applications are due. The report provides all the data foundations typically require.

Analytics Dashboard

  • Unique Volunteers — individual students who attended
  • Verified Hours — total hours across all events
  • Check-in Rate — registrations that showed up
  • Hours by Category — which categories your events serve

CSV Import & Webhooks

CSV Hours Import

Upload a CSV file with columns: Student Email, Event Title, Date, Hours, Description. Up to 500 rows per upload. Students not yet on ImpactBadge are saved as pending — their hours are applied when they sign up.

Webhook Integration

Connect your existing volunteer management system to push hours automatically:

  1. Go to Settings → "Generate Webhook Key"
  2. POST to https://us-central1-impacthub-prod.cloudfunctions.net/webhookListener
  3. Include your API key and an array of events (student email, title, date, hours)
  4. Rate limited to 10 requests/hour, 1,000 events per request

Webhook Security — HMAC-SHA256 Signatures

All webhook payloads are signed with HMAC-SHA256 for secure data exchange. When you generate a webhook key, ImpactBadge provides a signing secret. Each outgoing webhook includes an X-Signature-256 header containing the HMAC-SHA256 hash of the request body using your secret. Verify this signature on your server to confirm the request came from ImpactBadge and was not tampered with.

Web Portal

Access your full dashboard from any browser at impacthub-prod.web.app. Log in with your org admin credentials. The web portal includes:

  • Dashboard with stats and pending verifications
  • Analytics with charts and per-chapter breakdown
  • Volunteer CRM with search and detail view
  • Availability heatmap
  • CSV import tool
