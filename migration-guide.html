Migration Paths by Current System

From x2VOL

~20 minutes FREE

1

Export Student Data from x2VOL

Log into your x2VOL admin dashboard. Go to Reports → Export. Download the student hours report as CSV. This includes: student email, name, organization, date, hours, and verification status.

2

Format the CSV (if needed)

ImpactBadge accepts this CSV format. Rename columns if needed:

studentEmail,studentName,grade,organizationName,date,hours,description,category
alice@school.edu,Alice Smith,10,Richmond Food Bank,03/15/2026,4.5,Sorted donations,hunger
bob@school.edu,Bob Lee,11,Public Library,2026-01-20,2.0,Tutoring,education

Required columns: studentEmail, studentName, organizationName, date, hours

Optional columns: grade, description, category

Date formats: Both MM/DD/YYYY and YYYY-MM-DD are accepted.

3

Upload to ImpactBadge

Log into the ImpactBadge web portal as a school admin. Go to Import Students. Upload the CSV file. ImpactBadge will:

  • Match existing students by email → hours imported immediately
  • Pre-register new students → hours import when they create accounts
  • All hours marked as verified (pre-verified from x2VOL)
4

Invite Students

Students download ImpactBadge from the App Store or Google Play. If your school uses Clever, students sign in with one tap — no account creation needed. Their migrated hours appear immediately.

What carries over: All verified hours, organization names, dates, and descriptions. Students see their full history from day one.
What doesn't carry over: x2VOL-specific data like WBL/CTE tracking, supervisor signatures (replaced by ImpactBadge's own verification system).

From Helper Helper

~20 minutes FREE

1

Export from Helper Helper

Log into Helper Helper admin. Go to Reports → Student Hours. Export as CSV or Excel. The export includes student name, email, event, date, and hours.

2

Map Columns to ImpactBadge Format

Rename columns to match: studentEmail, studentName, organizationName, date, hours. Save as CSV.

3

Upload & Invite

Same as x2VOL: upload CSV in the web portal, invite students to download the app.

From Track It Forward

~15 minutes FREE

1

Export from Track It Forward

Go to Admin → Reports → Export All Hours. Download as CSV. Track It Forward exports include: volunteer name, email, organization, date, hours, and category.

2

Rename & Upload

Rename columns to match ImpactBadge format and upload via the web portal.

From MobileServe

~20 minutes FREE

MobileServe supports CSV export of student hours. Export from the admin dashboard, rename columns to match ImpactBadge's format, and upload via the web portal. Same 3-step process.

From Paper Forms / Spreadsheets

~30 minutes FREE

1

Enter Data into a Spreadsheet

If your hours are on paper forms, enter them into a spreadsheet (Google Sheets or Excel) with these columns:

studentEmail,studentName,grade,organizationName,date,hours
jane@school.edu,Jane Doe,9,Habitat for Humanity,09/15/2025,3.0
john@school.edu,John Smith,10,Food Bank,10/20/2025,4.5
2

Save as CSV & Upload

File → Download → CSV. Then upload via the ImpactBadge web portal.

Tip for large paper migrations: Start with current-year data only. Historical hours from previous years are less critical — students can self-report older hours with the self-report feature, which includes e-signature and photo evidence.

What Happens After Import

ScenarioWhat Happens
Student email matches an existing ImpactBadge accountHours imported immediately. Student sees them in the Hours tab.
Student email doesn't match any accountStudent is pre-registered. When they create an account with that email (or sign in via Clever), hours appear automatically.
Duplicate email in CSVEach row creates a separate hour entry. Multiple events for the same student are fine.
Invalid date or emailSkipped with error count. Other records still import successfully.
Student already has hours in ImpactBadgeMigrated hours are added to existing hours (cumulative). No data is overwritten.

Frequently Asked Questions

How long does migration take?

15-30 minutes for a typical school. Export CSV from your current system, upload to ImpactBadge, done.

Do students need to create new accounts?

If your school uses Clever, no — students sign in with their existing Clever credentials. Otherwise, students download the app and create an account with their school email. Their migrated hours appear automatically.

Will students lose any data?

No. All verified hours, organization names, dates, and descriptions are preserved. Students see their complete history from day one.

Can we run both systems during transition?

Yes. Many schools run ImpactBadge alongside their existing system for a semester before fully switching. New hours go into ImpactBadge while historical data remains accessible in the old system.

What if some students don't have email addresses?

For Clever schools, students don't need email — Clever Library SSO works without student email. For non-Clever schools, students can use any email to create an account.

Is there a cost to migrate?

No. Migration is free. ImpactBadge is free. There is no cost at any step.

Can we import from multiple systems?

Yes. The migration tool accepts a sourceSystem parameter. You can import from x2VOL, Helper Helper, Track It Forward, MobileServe, or any other system — just format the CSV to match.

What about Naviance integration?

ImpactBadge exports Naviance-compatible CSV files. Students can also export directly to Common App format — a feature no other platform offers.
