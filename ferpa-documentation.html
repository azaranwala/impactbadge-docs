1. FERPA Compliance Statement

ImpactBadge acts as a "school official" under FERPA § 99.31(a)(1).

Under the Family Educational Rights and Privacy Act (20 U.S.C. § 1232g; 34 CFR Part 99), ImpactBadge LLC operates as a school official with a legitimate educational interest when providing volunteer hour tracking services to K-12 school districts. This designation is established through the Data Processing Agreement (DPA) executed between ImpactBadge and each contracting school district.

As a school official, ImpactBadge:

  • Performs institutional services or functions that the school would otherwise perform itself
  • Is under the direct control of the school district with respect to the use and maintenance of education records
  • Uses education records only for the purposes for which the disclosure was made (volunteer hour tracking and related reporting)
  • Meets the criteria set forth in the school district's annual FERPA notification regarding school officials

3. Access Control

FERPA requires that access to student education records be limited to parties with a legitimate educational interest. ImpactBadge enforces this through Firestore Security Rules and Cloud Function authorization checks.

School-Scoped Access (isSameSchool())

The isSameSchool() helper function is called on every data access operation involving student records. It ensures that:

  • School admins can only view/export data for students enrolled in their school
  • Teachers can only view data for students in their assigned sections
  • Students can only view their own records and public school-wide data (leaderboards, events)
  • Parents can only view data for their linked child(ren)
  • Org admins can only view volunteer data for students who participated in their organization's events

Cross-school data access is not possible at the application level. Firestore Security Rules provide a second layer of enforcement at the database level, independent of application code.

Elevated Access Logging

Any action that exports or bulk-accesses student data is logged to the ferpaExportLog collection (see Section 4). This includes:

  • CSV/PDF report generation
  • Graduation tracking exports
  • Roster data views (bulk student lists)
  • FERPA-designated data export operations

5. Parent & Student Rights

Under FERPA, parents (and eligible students aged 18+) have the following rights with respect to education records maintained by ImpactBadge:

Right to Access

Parents and eligible students may request access to all volunteer records maintained by ImpactBadge. Data is available in real-time through the parent portal (for linked accounts) or upon written request to privacy@impactbadge.app. Requests fulfilled within 45 days per FERPA requirements.

Right to Amend

If a parent or eligible student believes volunteer records are inaccurate or misleading, they may request an amendment by contacting their school admin or emailing privacy@impactbadge.app. The school district makes the final determination on amendment requests, as ImpactBadge acts as a school official under their authority.

Right to Delete

Parents and eligible students may request deletion of their ImpactBadge account and all associated data. Upon receiving a verified deletion request:

  • All PII is removed from Firestore within 30 days
  • Firebase Auth account is permanently deleted
  • Volunteer hour counts are de-identified and retained in aggregate form only
  • The requesting party receives email confirmation upon completion

Right to Consent

For students under 13, COPPA-compliant verifiable parental consent is required before account creation. Consent records are stored in the parental_consents Firestore collection with parent email, timestamp, and consent method.

Contact: privacy@impactbadge.app

7. Breach Notification Procedures

72-Hour Notification Commitment: ImpactBadge will notify affected school districts within 72 hours of discovering a confirmed data breach involving student education records.

Breach Response Timeline

TimeframeAction
0-4 hoursIncident detected (via Cloud Monitoring alerts, Crashlytics, or manual report). Initial assessment and severity classification (P0-P3).
4-24 hoursContainment measures implemented. Affected systems isolated. Scope of breach determined: which data, which students, which schools.
24-72 hoursSchool district notification sent via email to designated FERPA compliance officer. Notification includes breach details (see below).
72 hours - 30 daysFull root cause analysis. Remediation measures implemented. Updated notification sent to affected districts with final scope and preventive actions.

Breach Notification Contents

Each notification to an affected school district includes:

  • Date and time the breach was discovered
  • Nature of the breach (unauthorized access, data exposure, system compromise, etc.)
  • Categories of data affected (names, emails, volunteer hours, etc.)
  • Approximate number of students affected
  • Steps taken to contain and remediate the breach
  • Recommended actions for the school district
  • Point of contact: privacy@impactbadge.app

Contact for Security Concerns

Report suspected data breaches or security concerns to: privacy@impactbadge.app

10. Annual Compliance Checklist

School districts should complete the following checklist annually to verify ongoing FERPA compliance with ImpactBadge:

  • DPA Review: Confirm Data Processing Agreement is current and signed. Verify purpose limitation and data minimization terms are unchanged.
  • Access Audit: Review list of school admins and teachers with ImpactBadge access. Remove any staff who have left the district or changed roles.
  • Export Log Review: Review ferpaExportLog entries for the past 12 months. Verify all exports had a legitimate educational purpose.
  • Subprocessor Review: Confirm the subprocessor list (Section 8) has not changed without 30-day prior notification. Verify all subprocessors maintain current compliance certifications.
  • Consent Verification: For students under 13, verify COPPA consent records are on file in the parental_consents collection. Follow up on any missing consents.
  • Data Accuracy: Spot-check a sample of student records for accuracy. Process any pending amendment requests from parents or eligible students.
  • Roster Sync Validation: If using Clever Secure Sync, verify that roster data (students, sections, teachers) matches the district's Student Information System (SIS).
  • Breach Response Test: Verify that the designated FERPA compliance officer's contact information is current in ImpactBadge's notification system. Confirm the 72-hour notification channel is operational.
  • Deletion Verification: Confirm that any students who graduated, transferred, or withdrew have been properly handled (accounts deactivated or deleted per district policy).
  • Annual FERPA Notice: Verify that the district's annual FERPA notification to parents includes ImpactBadge as a school official with legitimate educational interest, per § 99.31(a)(1).
