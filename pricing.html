Schools & Districts FAQ

How does the 2-month pilot work?

We set up ImpactBadge for one grade level or classroom at your school at no cost. We run an onboarding call together, help you create events and configure hour requirements, and you run it for 2 months. At the end, if you want to continue for the full school, converting takes one conversation. No paperwork needed to start.

Is there a minimum contract size?

No minimum for the School plan — you pay $1/student/year for however many students you have. District plans are custom contracts based on total enrollment across participating schools.

Do we need an IT approval process to get started?

If your district is already on Clever (which most are), your IT team simply approves ImpactBadge in the Clever App Gallery — a one-time action. Students can log in the next morning with their school credentials. No separate IT setup, no new passwords.

How is FERPA compliance handled?

ImpactBadge operates as a "school official" under FERPA, with legitimate educational interest. Student data stays in US-based Firebase infrastructure, is never sold or shared with third parties, and can be deleted on request. We can provide a full data privacy brief for your IT director on request.

What about middle school students under 13?

ImpactBadge has a built-in COPPA compliance flow. Students who indicate they are in grade 6–7 are required to provide a parent email address. A consent link is sent to the parent, and the student cannot access the app until consent is confirmed. This is baked into the onboarding — no additional work for you.

Nonprofits FAQ

Does my nonprofit need to create an account?

Yes — but it's very lightweight. You claim your organization using your EIN, we verify it against the IRS nonprofit registry, and you're set up in under 15 minutes. You'll have a login to create events and view your volunteer activity.

Do we need an app to approve student hours?

No. Approvals happen entirely by email — when a student logs hours for your event, you receive an email with one click to approve. You can optionally download the app for a richer dashboard view, but it's never required.

What student data can we see?

Only the information directly relevant to hours you need to approve: the student's first name, the event they attended, the hours they logged, and any comments they added. You never see grades, school records, or any other student data.

Can we share our events publicly on social media?

Yes — every event gets a shareable public link that anyone can view. To sign up, users must have an ImpactBadge account or log in with their school credentials. This keeps your event discoverable while protecting the sign-up data.

Students & Parents FAQ

Will this ever cost money for students?

No. Our business model is B2B — we charge schools and districts, not students or parents. Students are the core of the platform, and we're committed to keeping full access free for them permanently.

What if my school doesn't use ImpactBadge yet?

You can still use ImpactBadge as an individual student — sign up with your school email, log hours, find nearby events, and build your portfolio. If you'd like your school to join, share a link to this page with your school counselor and we'll do the rest.

Can I use ImpactBadge if I'm under 13?

Yes — with a parent's permission. During sign-up, if you indicate you're in grade 6 or 7, we send a consent link to a parent email you provide. Your parent clicks to confirm, and your account is activated. The app is fully usable after that.

How do I export hours for my college application?

From the Hours tab in the app, tap Export → choose Common App format, Naviance CSV, or a full PDF volunteer portfolio. All verified hours are included automatically with supervisor confirmation details attached.
